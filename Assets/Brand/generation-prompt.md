# Flashcard Reviewer logo

The final selected version uses a forest-green tile for stronger contrast.

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The PNG preserves transparency; the ICNS contains standard macOS icon sizes. Both Xcode and the standalone packaging script use the ICNS.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: Primary logo and macOS application icon for Flashcard Reviewer, a native app for reviewing, correcting and approving study flashcards.
Primary request: Create one polished, distinctive icon: a compact stack of three warm ivory flashcards with a bold forest-green approval check integrated into the front card. The cards have gently rounded corners, confident simple silhouettes, subtle offset layers, and no writing. The check is the central visual gesture, custom drawn with pleasing proportions and rounded ends.
Scene/backdrop: A warm cream rounded-square macOS icon tile, isolated on a genuinely transparent background outside the tile.
Style/medium: Restrained premium macOS icon design, precise vector-like geometry rendered as a high-quality bitmap, slight tactile paper depth and very soft shadows. Calm, scholarly, warm and modern.
Composition/framing: Square canvas, one centered icon tile filling about 88 percent of the canvas, consistent transparent margin. Card stack fills the central 65 percent of the tile. Bold readable design at small sizes.
Color palette: Match the existing app: cream #F4F1EA, ivory #FFFEF9, forest green #2E7052, subtle neutral #DBD4C7. Use the green to give the card silhouette contrast against the cream tile.
Text: None.
Constraints: One finished logo only. Actual alpha transparency outside the rounded tile. Clean smooth edges, coherent card anatomy, clear checkmark. No tiny details, no wordmark, no letters, no typography, no brain, no graduation cap, no sparkles, no mockup, no presentation sheet, no watermark.
